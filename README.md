## About

One sentence recalling a childhood summer evening: my grandmother calling me in from the yard by the smell of fresh rice, and running barefoot across the hot concrete, certain the whole world was waiting at her table.

The entry program (`main.ts`) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Every summer evening my grandmother called me in from the yard by the smell of fresh rice, and I ran barefoot across the hot concrete, certain the whole world was waiting at her table.

Concrete, honest, small detail over greeting-card sentiment: the smell of fresh rice, bare feet on hot concrete, and the ache that the world that waited at that table is gone now.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Deterministic, no network: it always writes the same 184-character sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
